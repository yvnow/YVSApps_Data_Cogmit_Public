However, a new issue occurs after clicking **Generate Cogmit Card**.

Investigate and fix only the generation action and its transition to the generated card preview.

## 1. Investigate Generate Button Behaviour

Inspect the Generate Cogmit Card button handler in `ShareCogmitModal.tsx` and the associated card-generation functions.

Verify:

- The button click handler executes.

Identify the actual failure point before modifying code.

- Display the generated Cogmit Card preview.
- Hide or transition away from the initial generation-options interface as appropriate.
- Keep the generated preview inside the existing preview container.
- Make Download, Copy Link and Share actions available.

The generated preview must appear without navigating away from the current Cogmit page.
